Who I Am

I'm a first-year B.E. Computer Science student with a relentless focus on cybersecurity, offensive security, and ethical hacking. My entry into this field wasn't structured — it was driven by a deep, persistent curiosity about how systems break, how vulnerabilities surface, and how skilled professionals think like adversaries to protect what matters.

Surface-level technology never satisfied me. I've always been compelled to understand what happens underneath — how networks communicate, how web applications fail, how authentication logic can be subverted, and how OSINT transforms publicly available data into actionable intelligence. That obsession led me to hands-on practice over passive learning.

What drives me is deceptively simple: whenever I encounter someone more skilled, more knowledgeable, or more technically precise than me, I don't want to stay behind. That pressure is fuel. I am at the beginning of a long journey — and I'm committed to building the kind of expertise where the work speaks louder than any title.
