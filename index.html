Who I Am

I'm a Second-year B.E. Computer Science Engineering student driven by a relentless focus on cybersecurity, offensive security, and ethical hacking. My entry into this field wasn't structured — it was fuelled by deep, persistent curiosity about how systems break and how skilled professionals think like adversaries to protect what matters.

Surface-level technology never satisfied me. I've always been compelled to understand what lies underneath — how networks communicate, how web applications fail, how authentication logic can be subverted, and how OSINT transforms public data into actionable intelligence.

What drives me is deceptively simple: whenever I encounter someone more skilled or technically precise, I don't want to stay behind. That pressure is fuel. I'm committed to building expertise where the work speaks louder than any title.
